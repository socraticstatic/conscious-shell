# conscious-shell

## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, prospective consulting clients (VPs who've been burned by AI
vendors), and design peers. They arrive from LinkedIn or a warm intro, usually
on a phone first, then return on desktop for the deep read. The job to be
done: decide in under two minutes whether Micah is credible, then find the
engagement offer or the contact path.

### Brand Personality
Blade Runner terminal noir. Operator, not showman. Warm-dark ground
(#0b0a08), bone text (#e8e4dc), one magenta accent (#e040fb) used sparingly,
cyan reserved for system chrome. Monospace as voice, not decoration - the
site is a shell session. Japanese subtitles as texture. Humor buried in
easter eggs (replicant endpoints, konami, verse), never in the sales copy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Desktop (full tier): ambient cinema - CRT, rain, HUDs, marquee. The costume.
- Mobile/tablet-touch (calm tier): a calm reader. Content first, no costume.
- The two tiers share typography DNA but mobile is OPTIMIZED for reading:
  larger body, looser leading, shorter measures - not desktop shrunk down.
- Anti-reference: generic dev-portfolio slop; glassmorphism; gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. Content renders real data from Supabase or it doesn't ship.
2. One accent. Magenta earns its rarity; the dock and HUDs stay quiet.
3. Text is never occluded. Fixed chrome owns an opaque strip or doesn't exist.
4. Mobile type is set for phones: >=16px body, line-height >=1.6, measure <=70ch.
5. The terminal conceit never wins against readability. When mono hurts small
   sizes, the label stays mono and the prose goes readable.
